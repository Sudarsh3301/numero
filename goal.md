## 10. Master Numbers

Master numbers are game changers — do not miss in prediction.

- **From Driver / Date:** can get 11 & 22
- **From Conductor:** can get 11, 22 & 33
- **Notation:** D= 2/11, D= 4/22, C= 2/11, C= 4/22, C= 6/33

### Calculation Methods

**Horizontal method** — add all digits of the full date:
```
29/02/1955 → 2+9+0+2+1+9+5+5 = 33 = 6  [Only 50% strength of master number]
```

**Vertical method** — add each date component column by column:
```
  2  9
  0  2
  1  9  5  5
  1  9  8  6  = 24 = 6  [Not a master number]
```

**Example — 18/04/1986:**
- Horizontal: 1+8+0+4+1+9+8+6 = 38 = **11** → C = 2/11 *(100% master number when both methods agree)*
- Vertical: 2, 0, 0, 9 = 11 = 2 → **100% master number**

**Example — 01/12/2005:**
- Horizontal: 0+1+1+2+2+0+0+5 = 11 = 2
- Vertical: adds to 2008 = 11 = 2 → **C = 2/11 100%**

**Notation:**  C = 6/33 50% vs C = 6/33 100% depending on method.

**Additional Info:**  
> 23/11/1999 = 2+3+1+1+1+9+9+9 = 35 = 8 (Destiny Number) — *8 will become active after age 35.*

> 2002 = 4 is **not** a master number.

### Significance of Master Numbers

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 11 | Dreamer, not achiever. With a support system, can be a doer. Must have a mentor. *(E.g., Amitabh Bachchan — always mentions his father, has 11 as master number.)* |
| 22 | Dreamer **and** doer. Once decided, no one can stop them. Creates own support system. *(E.g., Amit Shah.)* |
| 33 | Very very rare. Unconditional love, works towards humanity, preachers, spiritual gurus. |

> If two master numbers are present, always consider the **higher** number. E.g., if 11 and 22 are both present, consider 22.

#### Personal Month
Add the prediction month to the Personal Year:

> **Caution:** Add months 10, 11, and 12 as 10, 11, 12 — NOT as 1, 2, 3.

```
PY=3, Month=10:  3 + 10 = 13 = 4   → PM = 4
(Note: 4 is derived from 13, not directly from 3+1)
```

#### Personal Day
Add PY + PM + all digits of the prediction date:

```
PY=3, PM=4, Date=15/10/2027:
3 + 4 + 1+5+1+0+2+0+2+7 = 25 = 7   → PD = 7
```
